The problem

Primary teachers prepare for tomorrow while children learn at different levels. A score alone does not say what to reteach. Saathi closes the loop from a lesson plan to a paper check to the next action.

37–43

Children per pilot classroom; the three classes contained 38, 37, and 43 children.

What is new

PLAN→ CHECK→ NEXT STEPS
  • No student devices
  • Diagnosis and groups computed by code
  • Differentiated next lesson, not just a score

How it works

Six prompted agents are orchestrated by plain Python. Code handles marking, readiness maths, support-child selection, and arithmetic validation.
